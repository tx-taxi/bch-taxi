About bch.tx.taxi

bch.tx.taxi is the Bitcoin Cash explorer in the tx.taxi multi-chain family. Explore Bitcoin Cash blocks, transactions, addresses, mining activity and transaction fees.

Bitcoin Cash data

1 BCH equals 100,000,000 satoshis. Fees use satoshis per serialized byte: BCH has no SegWit weight discount. CashAddr and legacy addresses refer to Bitcoin Cash addresses. BCH uses ASERT difficulty adjustment each block, a 600-second target and 210,000-block subsidy halvings.
