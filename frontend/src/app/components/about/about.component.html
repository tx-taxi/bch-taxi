About bch.tx.taxi

bch.tx.taxi is the Bitcoin Cash explorer in the tx.taxi multi-chain family. It preserves Mempool's proven interface for inspecting blocks, transactions, addresses, mining activity, fee estimates, and the live Bitcoin Cash mempool while using a Bitcoin Cash-specific tx.taxi identity.

Bitcoin Cash data

1 BCH equals 100,000,000 satoshis. Fees use satoshis per serialized byte: BCH has no SegWit weight discount. CashAddr and legacy addresses refer to indexed Bitcoin Cash history. BCH uses ASERT difficulty adjustment each block, a 600-second target and 210,000-block subsidy halvings.

Coverage

Mining charts show the latest 15 indexed blocks. Hashrate is estimated from observed work and elapsed time. Mempool charts reflect this session’s observations of one node, not a global pool. CashTokens are separate from BCH amounts; their raw category, units and NFT commitments are shown when present.

Infrastructure

The explorer reads public Bitcoin Cash network data through its configured backend. Explorer results are informational and can differ from another node's view of the mempool. Always verify payment details in your wallet and use your own node when independent verification matters.
