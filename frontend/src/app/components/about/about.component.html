About btc.tx.taxi

btc.tx.taxi is the Bitcoin explorer in the tx.taxi multi-chain family. It preserves Mempool's proven interface for inspecting blocks, transactions, addresses, mining activity, fee estimates, and the live Bitcoin mempool while using a Bitcoin-specific tx.taxi identity.

Bitcoin-native data

Values remain denominated in BTC, satoshis, or the selected fiat currency. Fee rates remain sat/vB, transaction sizes remain vB or weight units, and all block, address, mining, and Lightning concepts shown by the interface retain their Bitcoin meaning.

Infrastructure

The explorer reads public Bitcoin network data through its configured backend. Explorer results are informational and can differ from another node's view of the mempool. Always verify payment details in your wallet and use your own node when independent verification matters.

Source and license

The maintained btc.tx.taxi repository is private. A reviewed source archive for the deployed release is available below. The upstream mempool/mempool source is available under AGPLv3, and this fork preserves those license obligations.
